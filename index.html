How to scan

  1. Lay the print flat on a plain surface that contrasts with its border (dark table for white borders, light table for black frames) in soft light.
  2. Fit the print inside the frame until the outline turns green. White, black, and patterned borders all work.
  3. Tap Scan, then hold the phone level and slowly circle it over the print — like stirring a pot — for a few seconds. The reflections drift to different spots and the app keeps only the clean parts. If it says the reflection is stuck, add a little tilt as you circle.
  4. It finishes on its own (12 frames or ~9 s). Tap Finish scan to stop early.

Green outline = border found and locked. Yellow = lost it briefly. No outline after a few seconds → tap ✢ and drag the corners yourself.
